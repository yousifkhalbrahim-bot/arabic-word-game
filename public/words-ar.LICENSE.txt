words-ar.txt — Arabic word list for لعبة الكلمات

Built from two open word-frequency lists:

1) FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (ar_full.txt, OpenSubtitles 2018)
   https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0):
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

2) wikipedia-word-frequency by Ilya Semenov (arwiki-2022-08-29.txt)
   https://github.com/IlyaSemenov/wikipedia-word-frequency
   MIT License, Copyright (c) 2015 Ilya Semenov

Changes: Arabic letters only, 2–10 letters (2-letter words only when very
common in both lists), a word must appear in the subtitles list (40+ times,
or 4+ times and 12+ times in Wikipedia), removed words with attached prefixes
(ال، و، ف، ب، ل، ك، س) when the bare word is more common, removed
inappropriate words, foreign names (scripts/wordlist-exclude.txt) and words
already in the game's core dictionary; added scripts/wordlist-include.txt.
This derived list is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Build script: scripts/build-wordlist.mjs, then scripts/clean-wordlist.mjs
